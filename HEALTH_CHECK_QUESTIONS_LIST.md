# Health Check Assessment - Complete Questions List

This document lists all questions in the health assessment for review and updates.

**Total Questions:** 30 (28 required + 2 optional)

---

## SECTION 1: PHYSICAL HEALTH (6 Questions + 2 Optional)

### 1. Energy Level (Scale 1-5)
**Question:** How would you rate your energy level throughout the day?
- Type: Scale (1-5)
- Required: Yes

### 2. Stairs Climbing
**Question:** Can you climb 2 floors of stairs without feeling fatigued?
- Type: Choice
- Options: Yes / No
- Required: Yes

### 3. Flexibility
**Question:** How flexible are you when bending or squatting?
- Type: Choice
- Options:
  - Very Flexible
  - Moderate
  - Somewhat Limited
  - Very Limited
- Required: Yes

### 4. Exercise Frequency
**Question:** How often do you exercise per week?
- Type: Choice
- Options:
  - Not at all
  - 1-2 times
  - 3-4 times
  - 5-6 times
  - Daily
- Required: Yes

### 5. Daily Steps
**Question:** What is your average daily step count?
- Type: Choice
- Options:
  - Under 3,000 steps
  - 3,000 - 5,000 steps
  - 5,000 - 8,000 steps
  - 8,000 - 10,000 steps
  - Over 10,000 steps
- Required: Yes

### 6. Height (Optional)
**Question:** What is your height? (cm)
- Type: Numeric
- Range: 100-250 cm
- Required: No (Optional)

### 7. Weight (Optional)
**Question:** What is your weight? (kg)
- Type: Numeric
- Range: 30-200 kg
- Required: No (Optional)

## Notes for Trainer

- **File Location:** `lib/questions.ts`
- **To Update:** Edit the question text, choices, or add/remove questions in the `QUESTIONS` array
- **Question Types:**
  - `choice`: Multiple choice with predefined options
  - `scale`: Numeric scale (usually 1-5)
  - `numeric`: Number input with min/max range
- **Sections:** physical, pain, lifestyle, mental, goal
- **Scoring:** Questions are scored in `lib/scoring.ts`
- **Recommendations:** Generated in `lib/recommendations.ts`
